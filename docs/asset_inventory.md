## What each pack contains

### Casual-mobile-Platformer Music Pack FREE
10 MP3 tracks at 192 kbps, 74–240 s, by **SunixMuz**:
`A Jewel Road`, `A mushroom`, `Beatuful Road`, `Bird Town`, `Casual Dancing`, `Chill Together`,
`Cyber Road`, `Dancing Friend`, `Beauty Night`, `bizarre Place`.
The copyright text states the free version is **CC BY 4.0** and requires the credit
`"Music by SunixMuz, licensed under CC BY 4.0"`, plus a link to https://www.youtube.com/@SunixMuz.

### Fredoka,Lilita_One
- **Fredoka** is a variable font (`wdth`, `wght` axes) plus 25 static instances
  (Regular/Medium/SemiBold/Bold in Normal, Condensed, SemiCondensed, SemiExpanded, Expanded widths, plus Light).
- **Lilita One**: one static `LilitaOne-Regular.ttf`.
Both under SIL OFL 1.1.

### kenney_board-game-icons
256 white SVG board-game glyphs (`Vector/Icons/*.svg`, fill `#FFFFFF`), 64 px and 128 px PNG
renditions in light/dark variants, a spritesheet and two AI files. No undo/eye/gear glyphs;
it does have arrows, dice (d2–d20), tokens, cards, chests, hearts (`suit_hearts`), locks, timers.

### kenney_boardgame-pack
Board game art: 6 dice faces × red/white (+ bordered variants), poker chips (blue/green/red/white
with side views), playing cards, player pieces in 7 colours, spritesheets, bonus tiles, SVG/AI sources.
Dice and chips are **64 × 64 px** PNGs.

### kenney_casino-audio
55 OGG clips: card fan/place/shove/shuffle/slide, pack open, chip lay/collide/handle/stack,
**dice grab / dice shake / dice throw / die throw**. Good source for dice-fidget sounds.

### kenney_digital-audio
63 OGG blips: laser, powerUp, phaser up/down, phaseJump, pepSound, threeTone/twoTone/tone, zap,
spaceTrash, high/low sweeps. Best source for bright positive/negative UI feedback.

### kenney_game-icons (1)
105 white/black PNG icons (1× = 50 px, 2× = 100 px) plus a white and a black spritesheet SVG/AI.
Covers audio on/off, music on/off, gear, home, trashcan, checkmark, cross, plus, star, locked,
information, shopping basket, pause, trophy, arrows. **No play, eye, vibration, gift or bulb glyph.**

### kenney_impact-sounds (1)
130 OGG impacts: soft/wood/metal/glass/plate/plank/tin/punch/bell hits and footsteps.

### kenney_interface-sounds (1)
100 OGG interface clips: click, back, bong, close, confirmation, drop, error, glass, glitch,
maximize/minimize, open, pluck, question, scratch (whoosh), scroll, select, switch, tick, toggle.
All clips are short (10 ms – 0.3 s).

### kenney_music-jingles
86 OGG stings in 5 styles (8-Bit NES, Hit, Pizzicato, Sax, Steel), 0.28–1.76 s each, plus a `Preview.ogg`.
All are short single-phrase stings, not full loops.

### kenney_ui-audio
52 OGG clips: click ×5, mouseclick, mouserelease, rollover ×6, switch ×38 (0.05–0.15 s each).

### kenney_ui-pack (1) — recorded, NOT used
1,315 files (4.2 MB): 870 PNG + 434 SVG widgets in Blue/Green/Grey/Red/Yellow (Default and Double
sizes, plus an "Extra" set), 6 OGG sounds, 2 TTF fonts, spritesheets and vector sources.
The DiceDoku UI is drawn in code, so nothing was copied from this pack.

### `puzzle-1-a.mp3` (loose file)
MP3, 57.66 s, 160 kbps stereo, 44.1 kHz, **no ID3 tags**. The filename suggests the OpenGameArt
"Puzzle Tune 1" (CC BY 3.0), but that cannot be verified from the file itself, and the game already
has three suitable loops, so it is **not used** and needs no attribution.
